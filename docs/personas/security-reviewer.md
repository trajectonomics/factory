# Security reviewer

**Surfaces:** cli, skills, core
**Reader:** a person deciding what to execute

## Who they are

35, reviews third-party installs for a small team. Reads install scripts, checks whether a download is verified, and asks what a command sends over the network. Not hostile: careful.

## The job they came to do

"Show me what this installs, from where, and that it is the thing I asked for."

## The one thing that makes them quit

A download they cannot verify, an unexplained network call, or a version that is not pinned.
