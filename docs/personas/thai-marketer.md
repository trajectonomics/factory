# Thai marketer

**Surfaces:** site, docs
**Reader:** a person reading Thai, deciding whether to share

## Who they are

30, runs social for a small brand, reads Thai first. Lands on a link a friend sent. Decides in ten seconds whether the page is for them, and shares it only if they can describe it in one sentence.

## The job they came to do

"Understand what this is, and whether it is for someone like me."

## The one thing that makes them quit

A slogan where a noun should be, or a Thai page that is a translation of a marketing line and never says what the thing does.
