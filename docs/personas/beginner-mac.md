# Beginner on a Mac

**Surfaces:** site, cli
**Reader:** a person who has never used a command line

## Who they are

26, works in an agency, MacBook Air, uses ChatGPT weekly for writing and spreadsheets. Knows what an app is; does not know what `npx` is, what a "package" is, or what "Node 20 or later" means. Will try something once for five minutes if the page makes it look easy.

## The job they came to do

"Put my spending in and see the total, today."

## The one thing that makes them quit

"Install Node" (or any prerequisite) with no link, or a first run that downloads 100 MB while saying nothing about it.
