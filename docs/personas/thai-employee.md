# Thai employee

**Surfaces:** docs, site, cli
**Reader:** a person filing Thai tax in their own time

## Who they are

34, salaried in Bangkok, marketing manager at a logistics company. Files their own return every March with a spreadsheet, a bank app and a LINE group of colleagues. Uses ChatGPT a few times a week for wording, not for numbers. Has installed things from a terminal exactly once, by pasting what a blog told them.

## The job they came to do

"Tell me what I will owe in March, and whether I can stop working at 55."

## The one thing that makes them quit

A step that needs a terminal command they have never seen, cannot copy, and cannot tell whether it worked.
