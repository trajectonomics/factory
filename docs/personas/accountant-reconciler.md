# Accountant reconciler

**Surfaces:** cli
**Reader:** a person who keeps books for a living

## Who they are

41, bookkeeper for three small businesses, lives in a spreadsheet. Knows double entry, knows what a trial balance is, distrusts anything that will not show its postings. Has seen five "simple finance apps" that could not reconcile a bank statement.

## The job they came to do

"Import last month's bank CSV and prove it balances."

## The one thing that makes them quit

An import that fails without naming the row and the column, or a report that cannot be tied back to the entries behind it.
